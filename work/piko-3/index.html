Mixed-media animated short

Piko 3

Chispa and 22 move through the memories of their bond, from quiet afternoons to the rush of the dance floor. Each shift in feeling brings a different texture to the screen.

Art direction

Chispa’s sharp orange silhouette and 22’s squared-off violet shapes carry through all four acts. The materials change with the mood, from cyanotype skies and drawn grass to molded plasticine and a cross-stitched title.

Materials and techniques

Cyanotype sky with clouds made from soap bubbles and acrylic on paper.

Grass modeled in Blender and composited with the cyanotype sky.

Stars made from crumpled paper above 3D hills.

A digitally drawn crowd and lights layered in compositing.

Scanned red ribbon, a cyanotype X-ray, and plasticine over acrylic.

Cross-stitch embroidery for the closing title.

Production

I co-directed the film with Nicolás Mesa and worked across production and art direction. We organized 2D animation, stop motion, and 3D backgrounds into parallel tracks so the team could test how handmade and digital elements came together in compositing.
